# Qortara Logic brand

The Qortara Logic logo is a metallic silver Q around a dark navy field, with a blue diamond of four nodes around a red center. It is the Qortara Logic product mark. The Qortara parent mark, a plain blue reticle with a red center, is a separate identity and is not used for this product.
